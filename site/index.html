HOW IT WORKS

Straight from the chain.
Signed on your machine.

01Reads Solana directly

pump.fun's own CreateEvent and TradeEvent from the program logs, the bonding curve's reserves, token accounts and launch metadata. No scraping, no pump.fun account.

02Builds, then signs locally

Trades are built by PumpPortal's local transaction API and signed on your computer with your own key. The key is never sent anywhere.

03Simulates before sending

Every transaction is simulated first. No SOL, bad slippage, closed pool: nothing is sent and your AI tells you why.

Read-only by default. Trading only switches on with PUMP_ALLOW_TRADING=true.
A hard cap per trade (0.5 SOL unless you change it), for buys and dev buys.
Your client asks before every call. Claude and Cursor show each tool call for approval.
